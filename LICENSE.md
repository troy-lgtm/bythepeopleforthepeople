## Upstream public-record content

Source URLs link to official records published by the relevant government
agency (CA LegInfo, LA City Clerk, Congress.gov, etc.). Those records remain
the property of the publishing agency under their own terms. We do not
relicense upstream content; we index and link.

## Bundled third-party datasets

- `united-states/congress-legislators` — public-domain dataset; see project
  for terms. We bundle a trimmed subset; original at
  https://github.com/unitedstates/congress-legislators
- `zippopotam.us` — used live, not bundled.
- US Census Geographies API — used live, public domain.
- US Census 2020 city populations — used in the bundled cities dataset,
